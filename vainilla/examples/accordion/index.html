Accordion

Panels that show and hide related sections of content behind a clickable summary, ideal for FAQs and grouped settings.

Basic usage

A <div data-mtx="accordion"> with data-summary generates the <h3> heading, the button with aria-expanded, the expand_more icon and the collapsible region. The content of the div becomes the details. The summary is bold (weight 500) and, when expanded, gets its own background with a divider that separates it from the content. Adjacent panels are grouped visually: only the first and last have rounded corners.

Adjacent panels share 1px separators, as in MUI.
Content of the second panel. When expanded it moves 16px away from its neighbors.
This panel starts expanded with data-expanded.
<div data-mtx="accordion" data-summary="Accordion 1">Content of panel one.</div>
<div data-mtx="accordion" data-summary="Accordion 2">Content of panel two.</div>
<div data-mtx="accordion" data-summary="Accordion 3" data-expanded>Starts expanded.</div>

From details

Progressive enhancement: a <details data-mtx="accordion"> works without JavaScript; once initialized it gets the MUI style, the height animation and the icon. The open attribute sets the initial state.

Does it work without JavaScript?

Yes. The native element opens and closes on its own; Materix adds the look, the transition and the group keyboard navigation.

Does the browser's find feature reach hidden text?

Yes: closed panels open automatically when Ctrl+F finds a match inside them.

<details data-mtx="accordion" open>
  <summary>Does it work without JavaScript?</summary>
  <p>Yes. The native element opens and closes on its own.</p>
</details>

Summary style

By default the summary uses medium weight (500) and, when the panel is expanded, a --mtx-color-action-hover background and a bottom divider to set it apart from the content in light and dark themes. data-summary-style="plain" (on the panel or on the group) restores the flat MUI look. Customize the result with the --mtx-accordion-summary-font-weight, --mtx-accordion-summary-expanded-bg and --mtx-accordion-summary-expanded-divider variables.

Gutters

States

Hover over a summary or press Tab to see the focus (action.focus background, as in MUI). data-disabled dims the summary to 38%, applies the disabled background and blocks interaction. data-ripple adds the optional ripple effect on press.

Exclusive group

data-mtx="accordion-group" keeps a single panel open (MUI's "Controlled accordion" pattern) and enables arrow, Home and End navigation between summaries. With data-exclusive="false" several can be open. The group propagates data-variant, data-square, data-elevation and data-disable-gutters to its panels.

Actions

A data-mtx="accordion-actions" child is placed at the bottom of the region, right-aligned with 8px between buttons (AccordionActions).

By continuing you accept the current terms of use and privacy policy.

  <p>By continuing you accept the terms…</p>
  <div data-mtx="accordion-actions">
    <button class="mtx-button mtx-button--text" data-mtx="button">Cancel</button>
    <button class="mtx-button mtx-button--text" data-mtx="button">Agree</button>
  </div>
</div>

Programmatic usage

The instance exposes expand(), collapse(), toggle(), the expanded getter/setter, disable() / enable() and emits mtx:accordion:change with { expanded }.

Content managed from JavaScript.

      
const accordion = Materix.getInstance(document.getElementById('accordion-api'));
accordion.expand();
accordion.expanded = false;
accordion.element.addEventListener('mtx:accordion:change', (e) => {
  console.log(e.detail.expanded);
});

// Create a new one
const el = document.createElement('div');
el.dataset.summary = 'New panel';
el.textContent = 'Content';
container.append(el);
new Materix.Accordion(el);
