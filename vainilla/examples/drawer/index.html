Drawer

Side panel for navigation or supplementary content: temporary, persistent, permanent or responsive, with a generated header and footer and an app shell pattern.

Basic usage

A data-mtx="drawer" container with data-title and its direct content. Open it with data-mtx-drawer-open="id"; it generates the header, the close button, the scrollable panel and the backdrop. Close it with Escape, the backdrop or the X.

<button class="mtx-button mtx-button--contained" data-mtx="button" data-mtx-drawer-open="drawer-basic">Open navigation</button>

<div data-mtx="drawer" id="drawer-basic" data-title="Mail">
  <nav data-mtx="list" aria-label="Main navigation">
    <a data-mtx="list-item-button" data-icon="inbox" href="/inbox" data-selected>Inbox</a>
    <a data-mtx="list-item-button" data-icon="star" href="/starred">Starred</a>
    <a data-mtx="list-item-button" data-icon="send" href="/sent">Sent</a>
  </nav>
</div>

App shell: AppBar + responsive Drawer

The main recipe for an application, with no custom CSS: a fixed AppBar, an mtx-d-flex row with the drawer data-variant="responsive" + data-clipped (temporary below data-breakpoint, permanent under the AppBar above it) and a <main> that starts with .mtx-toolbar-offset. The AppBar menu icon (data-drawer="id") opens the drawer on mobile and hides itself on desktop.

Variants

data-variant: temporary (default: modal with a backdrop, locks scrolling and traps focus), persistent (opens without a backdrop and the page stays usable), permanent (always visible: a fixed-width box in a flex row, with the panel sticky at the window height) and responsive (see "App shell" and "Responsive"). Here they are contained in a frame with data-absolute, which in permanent takes the container height instead of the window's.

Widths

Responsive default: min(85vw, 320px) on mobile, 280px from 600px and 300px from 900px. data-width="narrow|wide|full" sets 200px, 360px or 100vw, and any CSS length (450px, 40vw) is applied as is. It never exceeds 85% of the window width except with full.

Anchors

data-anchor="left|right|top|bottom". right is usually used for details or filters; top and bottom take the full width and fit their content (bottom sheet).

Header and footer

Without a title the drawer only wraps the content. data-title generates a text header; data-user-name, data-user-email and data-user-avatar, a user header. data-footer-version and data-footer-action (+ data-footer-action-icon) create a fixed footer; the middle scrolls its content independently.

States

Closed and open; you can also prevent implicit closing: data-disable-backdrop-click (neither the backdrop nor a click outside closes it), data-disable-escape and data-backdrop="false" (no backdrop). Triggers receive aria-expanded and aria-controls.

Under the AppBar (clipped)

The AppBar spans the full width and the drawer starts below it. On a full page use data-clipped on a permanent, responsive or persistent drawer: it is placed at --mtx-app-bar-height (56px on mobile, 64px from 600px) and stacks under the AppBar. The temporary drawer ignores clipped: it is modal and covers the AppBar, as in MUI. Here, inside a frame, it is achieved with a permanent drawer contained in the row under the AppBar.

Responsive: temporary on mobile, permanent on desktop

A single drawer with data-variant="responsive" and data-breakpoint="md" (sm 600px, md 900px, lg 1200px, xl 1536px): below it, it is temporary and opens with its trigger; above it, it is permanent and the trigger hides. When crossing the breakpoint, classes, ARIA, backdrop and focus are synced and mtx:drawer:mode-change is emitted. Resize the window.

Collapsible submenus

An item with data-mtx-collapse-trigger="id" and aria-expanded expands an mtx-collapse container with a nested list (data-nested). The mtx-collapse-indicator rotates 90°.

Programmatic usage

open(), close(reason), toggle() and isOpen. The log shows the real mtx:drawer:open and mtx:drawer:close events with their detail.reason: also try Escape, the backdrop and the X.


      
const drawer = Materix.getInstance(document.getElementById('drawer-api'));
drawer.open();
drawer.close('saved');
drawer.element.addEventListener('mtx:drawer:close', (e) => console.log(e.detail.reason));
