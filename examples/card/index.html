Card

A surface that groups content and actions about a single subject: header, image, text and buttons.

Basic usage Variants Header Media Actions Actions alignment Action area States Dark theme Product grid Programmatic usage Manual BEM usage

Basic usage

With data-title, data-subheader, data-avatar and data-image the card generates the header and the image. The content and actions are declared with data-mtx="card-content" and data-mtx="card-actions".

A Mediterranean dish with bomba rice, saffron, free-range chicken and fresh vegetables, perfect for sharing.
<div data-mtx="card" data-avatar="R" data-title="Shrimp and Chorizo Paella"
     data-subheader="September 14, 2026" data-action-icon="more_vert"
     data-image="paella.jpg">
  <div data-mtx="card-content">A Mediterranean dish with bomba rice...</div>
  <div data-mtx="card-actions">
    <button data-mtx="icon-button" data-icon="favorite" aria-label="Add to favorites"></button>
    <button data-mtx="icon-button" data-icon="share" aria-label="Share"></button>
  </div>
</div>

Variants

By default the card has elevation 1. data-variant="outlined" outlines it with a border, data-raised raises it to 8, data-elevation accepts 0–8, 12, 16 and 24, and data-square removes the radius.

Media

data-image generates a full-bleed 194px .mtx-card__media. By default it goes below the header; data-media-top places it at the top and data-media-height changes the height in pixels.

Actions

card-actions is a row with 8px of padding and spacing; its buttons take MUI's compact size and are right-aligned by default. Use mtx-card__actions-end to push an element to the right end while the rest stays at the start, and data-disable-spacing to remove the spacing.

Actions alignment

Materix aligns card actions to the right by default, just like dialog actions. This is a deliberate deviation from MUI, whose CardActions keeps them on the left: use data-align="start" to get that style back. It also accepts center and between, and data-actions-align on the card sets the alignment of all its actions.

Action area

data-mtx="card-action-area" turns the image and text into a single clickable zone with ripple, hover and focus, without affecting the actions. With data-clickable the whole card becomes interactive (role="button", Enter and Space). Hover or use Tab.

States

Normal, hover and focus (hover or use Tab) and disabled. data-disabled disables clickable cards and action areas: reduced opacity, no pointer and out of the tab order.

Elevation in the dark theme

In dark mode, as in MUI, each elevation adds a white overlay (--mtx-overlay-elevation-N): the card raised to 8 looks lighter than the elevation 1 card, and outlined has no overlay.

Programmatic usage

Card and CardActionArea expose disable(), enable(), focus() and the disabled getter; CardMedia exposes the image property. They do not emit their own events: listen to the native click (also fired by Enter and Space).


      
const card = Materix.getInstance(document.getElementById('card-clickable'));
card.disable();          // aria-disabled="true", tabindex=-1
card.enable();
card.focus();

const media = Materix.getInstance(document.querySelector('#card-action-area [data-mtx="card-media"]'));
media.image = 'new.jpg';
